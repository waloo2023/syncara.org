# Why Syncara

## The Problem

Creating synchronized subtitles, captions, transcripts, and lyrics is often a frustrating process.

Many tools are either:

* Too complex for everyday creators.
* Built for a single format only.
* Slow when handling large audio files.
* Dependent on cloud uploads.
* Limited when working with multiple languages.
* Designed around outdated workflows.

For professionals working with media, education, accessibility, localization, podcasts, lectures, interviews, or music, synchronization should be accurate, efficient, and reliable.

---

## Why We Built Syncara

Syncara was created to provide a modern synchronization workspace focused on three principles:

### Precision

Every word, subtitle, lyric, and segment should align accurately with audio.

The editing experience is designed to provide fine-grained control over timing while remaining intuitive and efficient.

---

### Performance

Large audio files should not slow down the creative process.

Whether working with short recordings or multi-hour projects, the experience should remain responsive, smooth, and predictable.

---

### Privacy

Your media and text belong to you.

Syncara is designed around privacy-conscious workflows that minimize unnecessary data exposure and keep users in control of their content.
