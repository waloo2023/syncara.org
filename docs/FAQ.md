# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Syncara?

Syncara is a professional synchronization workspace for subtitles, captions, transcripts, and lyrics.

---

## Who is Syncara for?

Syncara is designed for:

* Content creators
* Subtitle editors
* Translators
* Educators
* Media teams
* Accessibility specialists
* Researchers

---

## What file formats are supported?

### Import

* SRT
* WebVTT
* LRC
* TXT
* JSON

### Export

* SRT
* WebVTT
* LRC
* TXT
* JSON

---

## Can I work with long audio files?

Yes.

Syncara is designed to support both short recordings and long-form audio projects.

---

## Does Syncara support multiple languages?

Yes.

The interface currently supports:

* English
* Arabic
* French
* Spanish
* German
* Turkish
* Indonesian
* Hindi
* Portuguese

---

## Can I create subtitles?

Yes.

Syncara supports subtitle creation, editing, synchronization, and export.

---

## Can I create synchronized lyrics?

Yes.

Syncara supports lyric synchronization workflows and exports.

---

## Do I need special software?

No.

Syncara is designed to be accessible through modern web browsers.
