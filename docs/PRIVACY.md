# Privacy

## Privacy First

Syncara is designed around privacy-conscious workflows.

Your content belongs to you.

---

## Data Control

Users remain in control of:

* Audio files
* Subtitle files
* Transcript content
* Project data

---

## Minimal Data Collection

Syncara is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and reduce exposure of user-generated content.

---

## Secure Workflows

The platform is designed to provide:

* Reliable editing environments
* Protected user workflows
* Responsible data handling practices

---

## Transparency

We believe users should understand how their content is processed and managed.

Privacy is considered a fundamental product principle rather than an optional feature.

---

## Our Commitment

We are committed to:

* Respecting user privacy
* Reducing unnecessary data exposure
* Supporting secure creative workflows
* Maintaining trust and transparency
